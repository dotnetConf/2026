# More Processes, More Problems - how to speed up your builds with Multithreaded MSBuild

**Speaker:** Chet Husk

[Session details](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/en-us/conf/2026/agenda/brk008)

[Download the slides](multithreaded-msbuild.pptx)

Builds happen super frequently, so making this faster can have huge impact on your inner loops. Multithreaded builds can drastically speed up your workflow, and it's easy to try out! Learn how to try this new execution mode, what it means for your builds, and how to get future-proof your projects and tools to take advantage of it.
